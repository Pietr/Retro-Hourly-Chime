# Privacy Policy — Retro Hourly Chime

**Last updated:** 2026-11-17

**Retro Hourly Chime** is a standalone smartwatch mini-program for Zepp OS 3.0+ devices.

## Data Collection & Storage
- **100% Offline:** The app runs entirely on your watch and does not connect to the internet or any external server.
- **No Personal Data Collected:** We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information, health metrics, location data, or usage analytics.
- **Local Settings Only:** Your chime preferences (enabled channels, sound/vibration selection, active hours/days, DND and off-wrist settings) are stored strictly on your watch's local storage and are deleted automatically when you uninstall the app.

## Permissions Used
- `data:os.device.info` — Reads your watch screen shape/size and model name locally to adapt the UI and detect hardware features.
- `device:os.local_storage` — Saves your app settings locally on the watch.
- `device:os.alarm` — Schedules low-power hardware RTC hourly chime wakeups.
- `device:os.bg_service` — Allows the single-execution chime service to play your alert on the hour and exit immediately afterward.
